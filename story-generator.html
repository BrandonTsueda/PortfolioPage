Bratsu's Story Generator is a lighthearted, interactive app that lets you create your own short stories with just a few word prompts. Choose a genre such as Fantasy, Sci-Fi, Mystery, Adventure, Horror, Comedy, or Drama, then enter a noun, verb, adjective, and adverb to generate a personalized adventure.

This was one of my early programming projects, originally built in C# using Windows Forms. Recently, I revisited and modernized it with .NET 8, cleaner generation logic, a polished GUI, and a portable Windows build. It now includes genre-aware story templates, randomized prompts, dark mode, audio controls, copy support, and formatted .txt / .md export.

It's a simple but fun example of how code and imagination can come together. Whether you're trying it solo or with your kids, the Story Generator makes it easy to create something quirky and unique.
